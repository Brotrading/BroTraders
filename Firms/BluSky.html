BluSky Trading is a Florida-registered futures prop firm founded in 2022 by Mike Tolbert. Its flagship Propel Premium evaluation bills monthly until passed (not a one-time fee) across $50K–$200K accounts, with an end-of-day trailing drawdown, 30% consistency rule, and a 90% profit split once funded. BluSky also offers Instant Sim Funded and Direct-to-Funded options for traders who'd rather skip the evaluation entirely. Note: BluSky restricts traders from roughly 87 countries — one of the broadest restriction lists among futures prop firms — worth checking before signing up.
