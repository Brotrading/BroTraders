How we score

BroScore compares every plan with the others at the same size, from 0 to 100:

  • 35% total to get funded (price today + activation fee)
  • 15% profit target and 15% drawdown room, relative to account size
  • 10% drawdown type: static and end of day beat intraday trailing
  • 8% daily loss limit and 7% consistency rule: none is best
  • 10% payout split

Rules a firm hasn't published count as average.

New to prop firms? What the columns mean
Promo now
A discount running today, from the firm's website, its Discord or its newsletter, with the end date when the firm gives one.
Normal price / Price today
The regular price, and what you pay today with the promo. "/mo" means a monthly fee until you pass.
Activation fee
A one-time fee some firms charge when you pass and get your funded account.
Total to get funded
Price today plus the activation fee: what it really costs to reach a funded account if you pass on the first try. The fairest way to compare.
Other fees
Monthly renewals until you pass, reset prices and any monthly fee while funded.
Profit target
How much profit you need to pass the evaluation. Instant funding accounts have none.
Max drawdown
How far the account can fall before it fails. End of day only checks your balance at the close; intraday trailing follows your open profits tick by tick and is stricter; static never moves.
Daily loss limit
The most you can lose in one day before trading stops. "None" means no daily limit.
Consistency
Your best day can't be more than this share of your total profit. "None" means no such rule.
Payout split
The share of profits you keep once funded.
